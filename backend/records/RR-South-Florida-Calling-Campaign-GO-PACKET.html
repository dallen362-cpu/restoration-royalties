5 · Outbound opener — B2B (property managers / HOA / commercial)

Open“Hi, this is [Name] with Restoration Royalty — we're the local IICRC-certified restoration crew handling smoke, water and storm damage across [City]. With the recent smoke and storm activity in your area, are you the right person for property maintenance and restoration?”

Reason for call“We're offering property managers in [City] a free, no-obligation inspection — smoke/soot, moisture and roof — so you have documentation on hand before a small issue becomes an insurance claim. Takes about 20 minutes on-site.”

Qualify“How many properties/units do you manage in the area? Have any had smoke odor, water intrusion, or roof issues recently?”

Book“I can get one of our crews out this week for the free inspection and a written scope you can keep on file. What's the best day — and the best mobile number for the confirmation text?”

Rebuttal · “we have someone”“Totally fair — most managers do. This is just a free second set of eyes and documentation you own, with 24/7 response if something breaks after hours. No cost to keep us in your back pocket.”

Rebuttal · “not interested”“Understood. Can I at least text you our direct line so you have a 24/7 IICRC crew on hand the next storm or leak hits? That's the whole value — you're covered before you need it.”

6 · Inbound script (ad / site callers)

Greeting“Thank you for calling Restoration Royalty. This is [Name]. Are you calling about smoke damage, storm damage, or roofing help today?”

Confirm location“Can I get the property address or neighborhood so I can check your area and dispatch coverage?”

Qualify“Is this for a home or business? Have you noticed smoke odor, soot, haze, or roof damage? When did you first notice it?”

Urgency / safety“Is anyone having breathing issues, or is the property unsafe to stay in right now? If so, please move to a safe area and I'll help with the fastest next step.”

Frame“We're using weather-aware dispatch to prioritize the most affected locations first. For smoke, we help with cleanup, odor removal, surface treatment, and indoor-air recovery — and if roofing's involved, we bundle that inspection so it's one coordinated response.”

Close for dispatch“I can get this into the queue now — a quick inspection and a callback from our team with timing. What's the best mobile number for text confirmations? Updates by call, text, or both?”

Final“Thanks — you're in the system. If crew timing shifts, we'll text you right away.”

7 · Text intake · closer handoff · voicemail

Text — first reply“Thanks for contacting Restoration Royalty. Please send your name, address, and the main issue: smoke odor, soot, haze, roof damage, or all of the above.”

Text — follow-up“We'll review your location and dispatch priority, then reply with the next step and timing.”

Text — if urgent“If anyone has trouble breathing or the property feels unsafe, move to a safe location now and let us know immediately.”

Closer handoff“Based on what you've shared, this looks like a priority case. I'm transferring you to our closer now to lock in the next step.”

Voicemail“Hi, this is [Name] with Restoration Royalty. We received your request about smoke or storm damage. Please call us back at [number] and include your address so we can review dispatch priority.”

Rep rules: keep questions short · get the address early · confirm smoke-only / roof-only / both · always capture the best SMS number · calm, confident language · collect the facts before over-explaining.
