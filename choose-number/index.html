How it works — you pick, we provision, over their rails

👆
You pick, we provision

Real-time DID issuance the moment you choose — the number you clicked is reserved and lit as your live business line, not a request in a queue.

◈
Our portal over their rails

A white-label overlay of the carrier's number-ordering API — the same wholesale inventory, private-labeled as Telvergence, so the whole storefront looks and bills as ours.

📈
We set the markup

Our margin on carrier cost — transparent and ours to set. Cost plus our markup is your price; the spread is the business, and it's controlled from one dial.

🧾
Billing & collections included

Handled free under our private-label permission — the carrier runs invoicing and collections beneath our brand, so there's nothing for you to run.

📜
Backed by an executed wholesale agreement

This isn’t a pitch for access — Telvergence operates under a long-standing, executed wholesale agreement with the underlying carrier. The rails, the private-label permission, and the markup model are already ours to use.
