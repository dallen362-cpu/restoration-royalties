Executive Summary

One console. Every storm. Every trade. Fully automated.

The Weather Intelligence Network (WIN) is a real-time dispatch marketplace with the Telvergence telecom platform beneath it. Every partner receives a dedicated Smart-DID and takes inbound & outbound calls on that business number — while customer data stays in their own CRM. WIN captures storm-driven demand nationally, places each lead with an exclusive local partner, and every transaction trains a data engine that makes the next placement faster and cheaper.

📡 A carrier, not a lead list

Owned Smart-DIDs with local presence, STIR/SHAKEN, and keyword routing — leads ring real numbers, never a shared auction feed.

👑 Exclusive territories as assets

Each trade in each NPA·NXX prefix is claimable by one provider — a scarce, ownable, resellable franchise-style asset.

🔁 An Amazon-style flywheel

Demand pulls supply, supply widens coverage, coverage captures more demand — the data compounds every turn.

📈 Recurring + residual + exit

$22.95/line subscriptions, white-label royalties, and a proprietary data moat build a sellable, multiple-driven company.

The Engine

The flywheel — the business plan in one loop.

Amazon's flywheel: lower prices bring more customers, which bring more sellers, which widen selection. WIN runs the same loop on storm demand and local supply — with no weak link that stays weak.
