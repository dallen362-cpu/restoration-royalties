# Telvergence

> AI-receptionist and UCaaS platform: 24/7 speech-to-speech answering on local Smart-DID phone numbers, priced per answered line, per brand — from $229.50/mo — with a live network of running brands you can call.

## About

Telvergence provisions a business with a local "Smart-DID" phone number in its own rate center — branded caller-ID (CNAM), 411/e-411 and directory listings — then answers that line 24/7 with speech-to-speech AI that books jobs, logs recordings and transcripts, and feeds the CRM. The company's stated thesis: "A call from a local number in your own rate center gets answered. An out-of-area caller-ID gets ignored."

Pricing is per answered line, per brand — never per seat — month to month with a 30-day guarantee:

- Smart Line — $229.50/mo: one rate-center Smart-DID, 24/7 speech-to-speech answering, booking, registries, transcripts (the plan Telvergence's own live network runs on)
- Front Office — $499/mo: three lines, the brand's cloned voice, mid-call booking, CRM backfill
- Market Command — $999/mo: ten lines, AI-scored territory portfolio, vanity numbers, storm dispatch
- Network — from $2,499/mo, custom: white-label, multi-market, private encrypted lines
- Activation: $499 per brand, waived on annual

The platform is demonstrated by a live network of running properties — including Restoration Royalties (786-807-MOLD, Miami-Dade restoration), FAST Adjusting (855-2-ADJUST, claims intake), Coastal Crown (pressure washing), and YES Electrical (storm-ready dispatch) — real lines that answer when called.
