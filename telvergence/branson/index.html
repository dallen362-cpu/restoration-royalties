The battle cry

A local call, answered, is the whole game.

“A call from a local number in your own rate center gets answered. An out-of-area caller-ID gets ignored.”

A debtor in crisis at 2 a.m. dials a 407 number and a trusted local voice answers in their language — before fear talks them out of getting help. An out-of-area caller-ID would never get that pickup.

That is the Telvergence secret sauce: rate-center call origination and termination inside your own market — every ring local, trusted, and booked, on the Weather Intelligence Network.

The private client line — the new gold standard for law. Every client is assigned their own Smart-DID: a dedicated, encrypted direct line that knows their case file and persona, rings straight to their legal team, and is built to carry secure live video for remote hearings, depositions, and attorney conferences. Confidentiality by design, on a number that is theirs alone.
Port your number — and turn every collector call into evidence. Move your existing phone number onto the platform (or take a new Smart-DID) and every collection call is captured at the network level — the root PSTN call record: who called, when, and how often. Calls before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m., harassment by frequency, calls that continue after the firm takes your case — each one becomes documented, carrier-grade proof. Violations of the FDCPA, TCPA, and Florida's FCCPA can be turned into counterclaims, statutory damages, and fee-shifting — leverage that can offset, and in the right case eliminate, what a creditor claims. The phone that used to ring with fear starts building your case instead.
Recorded lawfully. Spoofing exposed. The line announces recording to every caller — Florida's two-party consent, satisfied by design — so each violating collector call becomes an admissible recording attached to the client's file. And because the platform sits at the network layer, every inbound call carries its STIR/SHAKEN attestation: spoofed or masked caller-ID — the collector's favorite trick — is flagged with carrier-grade proof. Spoofing to deceive is illegal under the federal Truth in Caller ID Act, exposing violators to five-figure forfeitures per call, on top of per-call civil statutory damages — and criminal exposure where the conduct crosses the line. The recording, the root record, and the spoof flag land in one evidence file your attorney can take straight to the fight — to void, offset, or turn the debt into their liability.
Built, not promised. This engine already exists: carrier-grade dialing, recording, and call-detail capture run on our production telephony stack, with the compliance rules programmed into the platform and its cloud backups, wired to the BOSS case-management CRM — an integration architecture first authored in 2019 and hardened across production ever since. Branson Ainsworth would not be beta-testing an idea; it would be plugging into a machine that has been doing this work for years.
