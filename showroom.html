The Engine

The Pitch & Catch Call Campaign

Branded local lines place the pitch; an AI-assisted intake desk catches every inbound — so the leads other shops miss land with you, documented and routed.

▶ The Pitch — Outbound

Reach the decision-makers first

  • Local-presence numbers by area code — not an 800 that gets screened
  • Branded CNAM so the name, not "Spam Likely," is what rings
  • Manual-dial posture to published business lines, suppression list enforced
  • SMS follow-up on consented numbers to warm the next touch
  • Each line mapped to a segment so attribution is clean
● The Catch — Inbound

Capture every call, book the appointment

  • AI answers & qualifies inbound around the clock
  • Structured capture: name → need → location → urgency
  • Safety-first triage; high-value opportunities handed to a closer
  • Call tracking ties each booking to the line that drove it
  • Appointment scheduled on the call, confirmed by SMS
