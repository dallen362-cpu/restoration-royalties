Your three rights

Any one of these can protect you. Together they are strong.

  • 1You are a real tenant

    You live at 8832 under a signed lease. Under the federal Protecting Tenants at Foreclosure Act (PTFA) you're entitled to at least 90 days' notice, and under Florida Chapter 83 a tenant can only be removed through a separate, proper eviction case — not by a foreclosure sale.

  • 2You are a disabled resident

    Because you are a person with a disability (SSI/SSDI), the Fair Housing Act lets us formally ask the court for a reasonable accommodation — to cancel or postpone the sale so your housing isn't lost.

  • 3You were never served

    You were never named, served, or notified in this case. Under basic due process (Mullane v. Central Hanover Bank), a person's rights can't be decided without proper notice and a chance to be heard.
