1. Verified Motion to Intervene and to Cancel or Postpone Foreclosure Sale

IN THE COUNTY COURT IN AND FOR ORANGE COUNTY, FLORIDA PHILLIPS LANDING MASTER COMMUNITY ASSOCIATION, INC., Plaintiff, v. CASE NO.: 2026-CC-001893-O UCN: 482026CC001893A001OX DAVID DREW ALLEN, et al., Division 79 Defendant. _______________________________________/ VERIFIED MOTION OF JOSHUA RAYMOND COBB TO INTERVENE AND TO CANCEL OR POSTPONE THE FORECLOSURE SALE SET FOR AUGUST 13, 2026

Joshua Raymond Cobb, a non-party resident and tenant of the subject property, respectfully moves this Court, pursuant to Fla. R. Civ. P. 1.230 (intervention) and section 45.031, Florida Statutes (and this Court's inherent authority over judicial sales), to permit his intervention and to cancel or postpone the sale set for August 13, 2026, and states:

Background

  1. Movant Joshua Raymond Cobb resides at 8832 Southern Breeze Drive, Orlando, FL 32836 (the "Property") as a bona-fide tenant under a written residential lease with the owner, David Drew Allen.
  2. Movant is a person with a disability who receives Social Security disability benefits (SSI/SSDI).
  3. A Summary Final Judgment of Foreclosure was entered July 2, 2026, and a judicial sale is set for August 13, 2026, at 11:00 a.m.
  4. Movant was never named, served, or notified in this action and learned of the sale only recently.

Grounds for Relief

I. Movant is a bona-fide tenant entitled to protection. Under the Protecting Tenants at Foreclosure Act (12 U.S.C. § 5220 note), a bona-fide tenant is entitled to no less than 90 days' notice, and under Chapter 83, Florida Statutes, a residential tenant may be removed only through a separate, proper eviction proceeding — not summarily through a foreclosure sale.

II. Movant is a disabled resident entitled to a reasonable accommodation. Under the Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. § 3604(f)), Movant requests, as a reasonable accommodation, that the Court cancel or postpone the sale so that his housing is not lost while his rights are determined.

III. Movant was denied due process. Having never been named, served, or notified, Movant's interest cannot be extinguished consistent with due process. See Mullane v. Central Hanover Bank & Trust Co., 339 U.S. 306 (1950).

IV. The relief is narrow and prejudices no party. Movant asks only that the sale be canceled or postponed and that he be heard; the Association's lien and judgment remain in full force.

Relief Requested

WHEREFORE, Movant respectfully requests that the Court: (a) grant leave to intervene; (b) cancel the sale set for August 13, 2026, or, in the alternative, postpone it not less than ninety (90) days; (c) require any rescheduled sale to be noticed anew under section 45.031, Florida Statutes; and (d) grant such other relief as is just.
