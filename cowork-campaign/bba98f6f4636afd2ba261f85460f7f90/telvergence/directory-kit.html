◆ 5. Google Business Profile — exact setup steps

Go to business.google.com → "Manage now." Sign in with the Google account you want to own the listing.

Enter the brand name (e.g. "Restoration Royalty"). Pick the category from section 3.

Choose "I deliver goods and services to my customers" → set service areas (from the NAP block). Enter your address privately when asked, then hide it.

Add the branded phone number + website (the catch-page link). Paste the long description + services.

Verify — Google mails a postcard (or offers phone/video). Enter the code when it arrives to go live.

Post weekly Google Posts (offers/updates) and set up a review link — send it to every happy customer. Reviews = free ranking fuel.

Honest note on automation: these listings can't be auto-created by a bot — each verifies your identity (postcard, phone, or email) to stop spam.
