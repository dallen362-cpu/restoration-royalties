# The FAST Standard — kitchen-sink feature parity for every gigapress brand

**Origin:** `fastadjustingservice.com` is the reference implementation — it carries features none of our
other material had. David's rule (2026-08-27): **every internal brand site (telvergence.com/<slug>), every
external brand site, and the telvergence.com master must reach FAST's feature set**, and these features are
**baked into the assembly line** so new brands inherit them automatically. Harvest the *features and
concepts* (below) — regenerate them on OUR brand, our URL, our issued Smart-DID (never a client's legacy identity).

## ★★ THE CENTRAL THEME (the purpose of every site — highest priority)
**Every subscriber is also a reseller, and every subscription is a business-in-a-box.** This is the core
purpose on EVERY site — the master, every internal brand, every external brand — not a side feature:
- **Subscribe → you're instantly in business.** The software + the issued Smart-DID + the digital business
  card make anyone an operating business in their industry the same day. Canonical example: **the drone app —
  any drone owner subscribes to our software (~$349/mo) and is instantly in the roofing-inspection business.**
  Swap the industry, same framework: instant entry, any vertical.
- **Every subscriber is naturally a reseller.** They already use it, believe in it, need it, and were trained
  on it — so they can speak about it intelligently, use it, and show others. So every subscriber is *endowed*
  with the ability to resell it and earn through their own issued DID.
- **The symbiotic, ubiquitous flywheel.** Each new subscriber becomes a new seller who signs up the next —
  a self-distributing network that compounds. Growth of the individual = growth of the network, and vice versa.
- **Frame the sale as an opportunity, not just a service:** the pitch is "subscribe, be in business today, and
  build income reselling the exact tool you now depend on." Every brand page carries this flywheel prominently.
- COMPLIANCE: reseller/referral earnings vary, are not guaranteed, and are subject to program terms + FTC/state
  rules; regulated verticals (adjusting, contracting) add the per-state licensing disclaimer.

## ★ The two signature concepts (never ship a brand without them)

### 1. Smart-DID issuance = the subscriber's Agent-ID + Royalty Account
A visitor gets **issued a real, verified company phone number in under a second, no human** — and that number
is simultaneously their line, their **agent ID**, and their **royalty account**. Copy pattern (regenerate per brand):
- "We provision and gift you a customized Smart-DID on your own digital business card — free."
- "It's your referral line, your agent ID, and your royalty account, all in one."
- Interactive **"▶ Provision a Smart-DID"** demo (idle → issues e.g. `(305) 555-0142` · "✓ Verified caller ID · assigned in 0.8s = Your Agent ID & Royalty Account").
- **"Claim My Free Company Number"** form (Name / Mobile / Email → "reserved the moment you submit").
- STIR/SHAKEN-verified branded caller-ID; the card is **provided and paid for** by the master company; personal cell stays private; **no license required** to hold a referral line.

### 2. The Master-Company Referral-Royalty model (pay subscribers through their issued DID)
The brand acts as the **master company** that **pays referral fees/royalties** to anyone who sends it business
**through their issued Smart-DID** — automatic attribution, no codes:
- "Refer [damage/leads] → earn a royalty, tracked and paid through the app, tied to your number."
- Trigger = job recovered/completed; **live royalty dashboard** (e.g. "12 Referrals · $4,850 Royalties · 3 Pending · Maria R. — mold job restored · +$620").
- **Field-fulfillment tier:** "No brand? No problem. Fulfill our leads. Keep 50%." (exclusive leads → your app, instant branding via issued DID, 50/50 gross split).
- **Ladder to ownership:** referral partner → 50/50 fulfiller → **$25K white-label / $25K+ franchise** (protected territory, branded app + Smart-DID engine + tracking portal + form inventory; ongoing software royalties).
- COMPLIANCE (absolute): consumer referral/commission and public-adjusting/contractor-referral rules are
  **state-regulated** — every royalty/referral surface carries "concept · verify licensing & legality per state
  before launch" and "information request, not an offer." No guaranteed-earnings claims.

## The interactive proof modules (the rest of the kitchen sink)
Every brand build should offer these, themed to its industry (regenerate labels per vertical):
1. **AI voice concierge** ("Ava"-class) — tap-to-hear + ask-anything + action buttons (already the Line Concierge standard).
2. **Multi-step Secure Intake** — 5-step guided form (type → status → property → contact → availability → confirm "reserved").
3. **Photo upload → Instant AI Assessment** — drag/drop photos → "Generate My Instant Assessment" → preliminary scope + a tracking number, under a 100% satisfaction badge.
4. **Real-time job/claim tracking** — enter a tracking number → live status bar + "🔴 Live activity" + "Install App" (demo: `FAST-2026-0001`).
5. **Form/inventory lock-match** — "select your [insurer/vendor] → ⚡ Instant Lock-Match" (industry analog: the right form/SKU/kit in minutes).
6. **Storm/event alert simulator** (WIN TV) — "▶ Simulate a Storm Alert" → detect → snap before → snap after = indisputable proof.
7. **QR Inventory** (patent-pending framing) — scan assets → instant, carrier-accepted logging.
8. **Member Portal** — sign-in (email/company-number + password, "keep me signed in", forgot-password) AND **sign-up that issues the Smart-DID** ("Create Account & Get My Smart-DID" → "your agent ID & royalty account", bank-level-encryption badge).
9. **Real-time multi-party collaboration thread** — one live thread: operator + crew + client + job status.
10. **Reviews → auto-post to Google** — star rating + review form + "⭐ Log & Post to Google".
11. **Direct-line rack** — "Call the right specialist — 10 dedicated lines," one per intent (maps to the 10-line Smart-DID grid).
12. **Careers / Now Hiring** + **Franchise/White-Label request forms** ($25K tiers).
13. **Live weather satellite/Doppler radar** — embedded over the brand's rate-center territory (Windy
    embed, free, no key; per-brand `geo` block: lat/lon/zoom/label). The WIN tie-in: storms are demand;
    the network watches the sky the phones answer to. Baked into `build.py` (`weather_radar()`);
    suppress per-brand with `_noWeather`.
14. **Closest-Starbucks courtesy map** — a directions map to the nearest Starbucks in the SAME rate
    center (NPA-NXX locality) as the client's Smart-DID (`geo.rateCenterLocale`, e.g. "Perrine, FL").
    Local-presence proof + hospitality nod: "we're local because your number is," one-tap directions.
    Baked into `build.py` (`local_courtesy()`); suppress with `_noCourtesy`. Always carries the
    trademark/no-affiliation fineprint.
15. **BOSS CRM + LMS back office (UCCPXaaS back end)** — every brand states and runs on the platform's
    own back end: **BOSS** (Back Office Sales Support, 2013→present, dialer-integrated 2019) — accounts
    keyed to the Smart-DID (never SSN), leads/pipeline/dispositions/call-log auto-captured; the **LMS**
    that trains and certifies every subscriber (the literal mechanism of the flywheel — "trained on it"
    → confident reseller); and **our own predictive-dialer core** (provider-agnostic wording — NEVER
    name the dialer software on a page). Data model of record: `backend/BOSS-CRM-LMS-SCHEMA.md`; live
    demo in the members showcase (cowork back office). Baked into `build.py` (`backoffice()`);
    suppress with `_noBackoffice`.
16. **Auto-vanity rate-center inventory** — at press time the gigapress AUTO-RUNS the vanity-DID
    generator and shows the best vanity Smart-DIDs in the brand's OWN NPA (area code/exchange),
    each labeled **"viable · not yet purchased"** — the live-inventory feel, honestly labeled
    (examples pending carrier reservation, not owned numbers). Config per brand:
    `"vanity": {"npa":"786","industry":"<BANKS key>"}` or `{"words":[…]}`. Baked into `build.py`
    (`auto_vanity()` importing `vanity-did-generator/scripts/vanity.py`); suppress with
    `_noAutoVanity`. Upgrade slot: when Vitelity API creds exist (env/secrets, never in repo),
    the same hook can verify true availability live before display. This is the 3T/3E in action —
    teach them to fish the numbering plan itself.
17. **WIN Official Essential Service Provider Partner seal** — every brand carries the WEATHER
    INTELLIGENCE NETWORK official-partner designation, "engineered for its NPA·NXX grid": the seal
    band names the brand, its NPA, and its territory, tying together the Smart-DID grid, the live
    Doppler telemetry (module 13), and cross-network storm dispatch. Wording discipline: it is a
    **Telvergence network credential, not a government license**; storm response = dispatch
    coordination, "in an emergency call 911"; never put the designation in a GBP business NAME
    (description/services only). Baked into `build.py` (`win_seal()`); suppress with `_noWinSeal`.
    Master site carries the network-wide version (#winseal band + concierge entry).

## House rules for applying it
- **OUR material:** brand name, URL (`telvergence.com/<slug>` or the brand's own domain we control), and the
  **issued Smart-DID** are ours; old sites are SEO/feature *knowledge* input only.
- **Provider-agnostic & honesty:** never name the AI/carrier vendor on a page; label live-demo assistants as
  demos until the LLM/telephony backend is wired; no invented stats; every regulated program (royalties,
  franchise, "patent-pending") carries its concept/verify-per-state disclaimer.
- **Gigapress propagation:** these modules live in the shared template (`brand-kit/build.py`) + `brand.css`,
  toggled/parameterized per brand via `brands/<slug>.json`, so a single build lands them on every internal
  brand. The telvergence.com master (V4) carries them as the platform's own offering. External brand sites
  (separate repos) are brought to parity as a follow-on using the same regenerated modules.
- **Listing tie-in:** the issued Smart-DID is the vanity keyword DID from `vanity-did-generator`, and it is the
  number recorded in the brand's GBP `gbp` block (see `listing-foundation`). One number: agent ID, royalty
  account, GBP line, and SEO keyword.

## Definition of done (feature-parity)
A brand is FAST-Standard when it offers: the Smart-DID **issuance demo + claim-your-number form**, the
**referral-royalty / master-company** band with a live royalty dashboard, the **50/50 + $25K** ladder, the
**member portal that issues the DID**, and at least the intake / photo-assessment / tracking / review-to-Google
modules — all in the house design system, all disclaimered, all on our issued number.
