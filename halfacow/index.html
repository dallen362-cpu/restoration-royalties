The Concept

One animal.
Nose to bun.

Half a Cow isn't another burger truck. It's a chef-driven premium-beef program on wheels — whole-animal sourcing, dry-aged flavor, and a tight, crave-able menu that travels. Built so a great cook can run a great business, and so a great operator can run ten.

🥩

Whole-animal sourcing

Buy better by buying the whole animal — smash blend from the chuck, steak sandwiches off the loin, brisket bowls from the plate. Less waste, deeper flavor, better margins.

🔥

A menu that travels

Smash-forward and fast, but chef-level: a signature blend, a house sauce, live-smashed to order. Simple enough to run from a truck, good enough to line up for.

👩‍🍳

Chef-driven, systemized

Recipes, prep, and plating are standardized into a playbook — the reason it can be a franchise, not a one-off. Consistency city to city, truck to truck.

Why it scales — the Telvergence flywheel

A truck is a job.
A network is a business.

Most food trucks stall at one truck because the owner IS the business — the phone, the bookings, the marketing, the leads. Half a Cow franchisees plug into the Telvergence platform from day one, so the network does the heavy lifting and every operator can grow.

1

Protected territory

Each operator owns a defined metro/market — engineered from real demand and event density, not drawn on a napkin. No cannibalizing your neighbor.

2

One Smart Number

A single memorable phone line per market — answered 24/7, in the caller's language — that books catering, events, and truck stops without the owner glued to a phone.

3

A branded site per operator

Every franchisee gets this exact gold-standard site, auto-skinned to their city — menu, schedule, booking, reviews, voice concierge — spun up in minutes, not months.

4

Event & festival dispatch

The network routes catering leads, festival slots, and private events to the nearest available truck — turning idle hours into booked revenue.

5

The referral royalty engine

Operators and fans refer new markets and earn — a self-feeding growth loop that recruits the next city for you. The brand distributes itself.

6

One playbook, one back office

Sourcing, recipes, training, scheduling, and reporting run on shared rails — so an operator can go from one truck to a small fleet without reinventing the wheel.

The Franchise

Three ways to own it

A ladder from first-truck operator to area developer — everyone on the same brand, the same rails, the same network effect. Structure shown is conceptual; actual terms are defined in a formal disclosure document.

Rung 1

Owner-Operator

One truck, one territory. You run the flat-top; the platform runs the phone, the site, and the bookings. The fastest path in.

Rung 2

Fleet Operator

Multiple trucks across your metro, a shared commissary, and a bench of trained cooks. You manage operators; the network feeds demand.

Rung 3

Area Developer

Develop a whole region — recruit and support operators, earn on the network you build. The Telvergence royalty flywheel, at scale.
