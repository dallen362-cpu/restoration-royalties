The architecture

Every franchisee gets their own city URL

The network runs on a master + per-city URL architecture — the exact same way restorationroyalties.com runs its sub-brands. The master brand holds the identity; each city operator gets a clean, memorable branded address of their own.

halfacow.com/dallas/ halfacow.com/philadelphia/ halfacow.com/<city>/
Master

One brand at the top

The master brand owns the name, the standard, and the platform. Every city storefront inherits the look, the voice, and the tooling — so a new operator launches as a credible national brand from day one, not a lone truck.

Per city

The stadium's own grid

Each franchisee gets their own city path — their private-label storefront and the Smart-Number territory that fans out from their NFL team's stadium, so the truck owns the game-day and tailgate demand while standing on the master's shoulders.

Proven

The same pattern, live today

This is not theoretical. The identical master-plus-sub-brand pattern already runs in production on restorationroyalties.com. Half a Cow is that architecture, re-skinned for premium beef on wheels.

Note on the address. Until the halfacow.com domain is purchased and pointed, this concept is served under the current path restorationroyalties.com/halfacow/… When the real domain goes live it maps 1:1 — /halfacow/dallas/ becomes halfacow.com/dallas/, unchanged.
