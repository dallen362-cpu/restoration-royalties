Good To Know

Questions Customers Ask Us

How much does it cost to book the truck?

Illustrative catering ranges run roughly $18–$28 per head for drop-offs and private parties, with festival, wedding, and large-scale bookings quoted as a flat fee plus per-head. A backyard BBQ is at the low end; a full wedding or festival activation is at the high end. Every range here is an illustrative estimate — your written quote confirms the exact price. Half a Cow is a concept, so no live pricing is being sold today.

Where does Half a Cow operate?

Half a Cow is a national food-truck franchise concept designed to roll out city by city — Miami first, America next. Each market is a protected territory served by a local operator. As a concept, no trucks are on the road yet; founding-market conversations are how it starts.

Is this a real business I can order from today?

Not yet — Half a Cow is an illustrative concept built on the Telvergence gigapress to show what a premium-beef food-truck franchise could look like. The menu, prices, territories, and Smart Numbers shown are conceptual and subject to change. Nothing here is an offer to sell food or a franchise.

What's on the menu?

A tight, high-craft lineup that runs fast off a flat-top: the Half-Pound Smash, the Ribeye Melt, brisket & burnt ends, the Butcher's Dog, and beef-fat fries. Everything is premium, whole-animal beef. Final menu and pricing are set by the culinary team per market.

How do I own a Half a Cow truck?

The concept lays out a three-rung ladder: Owner-Operator (one truck, one territory), Fleet Operator (multiple trucks across a metro), and Area Developer (develop a whole region). A franchise can be offered only through a registered Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) — the structure shown is conceptual, and no franchise is being offered or sold on this page.

What does it take to invest?

Because Half a Cow is a concept, there are no investment figures, fees, or earnings to quote — and no revenue, income, or profit is claimed or implied anywhere on this page. Any real franchise terms would be defined and disclosed only through a formal FDD. Founding-market talks start with your city and your kitchen background, not a number.

How does the Smart Number work?

The concept gives each market a single memorable Smart Number — answered 24/7, in the caller's language — that books catering, events, and truck stops without the owner glued to a phone. The grid shown (833-HALF-COW) is an illustrative concept, not yet provisioned or live.

Can you do festivals and big events?

Yes — that's core to the model. Fairs, festivals, breweries, and sporting events create demand rushes, and the network routes those catering and festival leads to the nearest available truck instead of letting them die in a voicemail box. As a concept, dispatch is a designed capability, not a live service yet.
