The platform · Designed capability

The six pillars

Cow Connect is not one feature — it is the operating layer every Half a Cow truck runs on, whether that truck is a single owner-operator or one of ten in a city fleet.

01

Call Command Center

Every city's Smart Number answered around the clock, in the caller's language, and routed to the right truck — so no game-day call, catering inquiry, or walk-up order goes unanswered.

02

Storm & Event Dispatch

Game days, festivals, weather shifts: leads and booking slots routed to the nearest available truck in real time, so demand finds the truck that can actually serve it.

03

WIN Weather & Event Intelligence

The network reads the calendar and the sky, flagging demand before it hits — kickoff times, weather swings, and local events surfaced as a heads-up, not a surprise.

04

Booking & Catering Engine

Private parties, corporate lunches, tailgates — booked and tracked from the first tap to the truck showing up, with nothing falling through a spreadsheet crack.

05

The App + SIP / Web / Phone

One operator app ties it together — calls, texts, bookings, and truck tracking on any device, so the owner isn't juggling four different tools to run one truck.

06

Automation & Reporting

Routing, follow-ups, and the numbers that matter run in the background — so the owner can run the truck without staying glued to a phone all day.

Designed capability, not a live system. Smart Numbers, call routing, dispatch, and the AI-assisted answering described above are capabilities designed into the platform and activate on provisioning for a live franchisee. Nothing above is currently answering live calls on this concept preview.

How a game day runs

A short walk-through of what the platform is designed to do on a home-game Sunday — a picture of the intended workflow, not a report of an actual event.

  1. ☀
    Morning · demand read

    The network reads the day

    Kickoff time, weather, and nearby events are checked against the city's trucks, flagging where demand is likely to concentrate before the first call comes in.

  2. 📍
    Pre-game · dispatch

    Trucks get their lane

    Tailgate bookings and walk-up zones are routed across the available trucks in the stadium's rate center, so no two trucks are fighting the same lot and no lot is left uncovered.

  3. 🔥
    Kickoff to final whistle · the rush

    Calls and orders keep moving

    Inbound calls and catering requests are answered and routed continuously through the rush, with each truck's queue kept realistic instead of overbooked.

  4. 📊
    Evening · wrap

    The day rolls into the numbers

    Calls handled, bookings closed, and follow-ups owed are summarized automatically — so the owner starts Monday with a read on the day, not a pile of notes to reconstruct it.

Why the platform matters

From one truck to a network

A single truck can be run out of a notebook. A second truck can't — and a tenth truck across a whole NFL city definitely can't. Cow Connect is what makes the 2nd, 5th, and 10th truck repeatable, not just possible.

One truck needs a hustler. A fleet needs a platform.

Owner-Operator

One truck, one city path, the full platform running underneath — Cow Connect handles the calls and the calendar so the owner can run the grill.

Rung 2

Fleet Operator

Multiple trucks in one territory, dispatched from the same command center — the platform is what lets one owner run several trucks without several separate systems.

Rung 3

Area Developer

A whole NFL-city network, coordinated across trucks and operators on one shared platform — the same engine, scaled to a market instead of a lot.
