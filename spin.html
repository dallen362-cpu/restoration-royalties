The Engine

The Pitch & Catch member-acquisition flow

Branded local lines place the pitch; an AI-assisted intake desk catches every inbound — so the rider who called at 9pm still gets booked into Saturday's 8am.

▶ The Pitch — Outbound

Fill the seats before class opens

  • Win-back texts to lapsed members ("we saved your bike")
  • Waitlist blasts when a spot opens in a sold-out ride
  • First-Ride-Free invites to local leads & walk-bys
  • Corporate & team-ride outreach to nearby offices
  • New-studio / franchise launch campaigns
● The Catch — Inbound

Answer every call, book the ride

  • AI answers & qualifies: new rider / member / event
  • Structured capture: name → class → date → shoe size
  • Books directly to the schedule & texts a confirmation
  • After-hours calls remote-forward to the on-call coach
  • CDR ties each booking to the line & flyer that drove it
