The engine

How a ring becomes a job

Five stages, none of them optional. The whole system exists so that no call ever reaches a voicemail box.

The four questions — are you the owner, do you have insurance, has a claim been filed, what is the damage — decide the route before a technician is ever dispatched.

  1. CaptureEvery entry point — the site, an ad, the storm console, a tapped digital card — is a tracked Smart-DID. The source of the call is known before anyone says hello.
  2. Answer, alwaysIntake picks up around the clock. Nights, weekends, and the middle of a hurricane are exactly when restoration calls come in, and they are exactly when competitors let them ring out.
  3. Qualify and routeOwner-occupied with coverage goes straight to scheduling. Everything else is captured, tagged, and worked later instead of lost.
  4. Book the assessmentFree moisture, mold, and roof assessment with a written scope. Low friction to say yes — and the written scope is what actually converts to signed work.
  5. Document and closeEvery call recorded, every scope written, everything packaged the way an insurer expects it. That packaging is the difference between a paid claim and a fight.
