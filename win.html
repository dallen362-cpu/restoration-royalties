The Engine

Storm-triggered Pitch & Catch

Branded local lines place the pitch the moment a ZIP goes hot; an AI-assisted desk catches every inbound — so the storm leads other shops miss land with the network, documented and routed.

▶ The Pitch — Outbound

Reach the affected first

  • Property owners in the hail/wind footprint — local, named call
  • Area roofing owners & crews for overflow and referral swaps
  • Insurance agents, brokers & public adjusters on storm claims
  • Adjacent trades — gutters, solar, GC, tree & tarp
  • White-label lead-gen resale to operators (B2B2B)
● The Catch — Inbound

Capture every call, book the roof

  • AI answers & qualifies: hail / wind / leak / full replacement
  • Structured capture: name → address → damage → urgency
  • Safety-first triage; large or insurable losses handed to a closer
  • Per-DID call tracking ties each job to the line that drove it
  • Drone assessment & estimate scheduled on the call

The Infrastructure

The Telvergence Smart-DID model

Branded local numbers, provisioned and routed by API — the anti-spam, fully-tracked backbone that makes storm-triggered outreach actually connect.

🏟️

Branded local numbers

Real local-presence DIDs by area code (407 / 321 / 561 / 954 / 305 / 786) with CNAM set to your campaign name — local and named, not "Spam Likely."

🛡️

CNAM + STIR/SHAKEN

Caller-ID branding plus STIR/SHAKEN attestation so calls land verified — the fix for spam-flagging and low answer rates.

📱

SMS & remote forward

Two-way SMS on every line and remote forwarding to any crew phone — the office follows the roofer, not the other way around.

📡

Per-DID CDR tracking

Call-detail reporting per line and per agent; spot a flagged number and rotate it instantly to keep deliverability high.

🤖

AI intake

Every inbound answered, qualified, and triaged by AI; large or insurable losses routed straight to a human closer.

📝

Written guarantee + QA

Month-to-month carrier — no lock-in — backed by a written satisfaction guarantee and recorded QA on the campaign.
