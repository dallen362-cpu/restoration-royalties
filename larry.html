The moat — six things we control that competitors can't

Vapi, ElevenLabs and GoHighLevel bolt AI onto calls but rent the phone layer. Telvergence owns it. That ownership is the margin and the defensibility.

1. Owned carrier numbers (DIDs)

Real local, toll-free & vanity numbers on our account — assigned, routed, and kept by us. Not resold.

2. Real-time DID provisioning API

Spin up a branded local number in minutes via the Vitelity API. The AI-only players simply can't do this.

3. The trunk & routing

We own the call path, caller-ID, CNAM, and failover — we decide where every call goes.

4. Port control (the deepest moat)

A port-out PIN/freeze means no one can move our numbers. Numbers are the asset; locking porting makes them permanently ours.

5. Wholesale margin

Buying at carrier wholesale means every resold line carries margin only we have access to.

6. The engine + the data

The dialer, the campaigns, the leads, the recordings and CDRs — all on infrastructure we own.
